# Graduated disclosure of an ATTRIBUTE section has no verified example anywhere in this corpus, and may not be expressible for heti-issued credentials. MEASURED on the pinned fork (bakobo/keripy@a1f2f32b) while migrating award (@jruwvxnt): (1) an acdcmap-built ACDC carries NO block 'd' fields -- @enr3eg recorded this for a.d and @xuq4lelk found the same for e.d -- and a d-less section is fully expanded during most-compact computation, so the top-level SAID is computed over the expanded content; (2) replacing any section or nested block of such a credential with its SAID and re-makifying yields a DIFFERENT top-level SAID (measured for a, r, and a.details), so compaction is NOT disclosure-invariant for these credentials; (3) SerderACDC refuses a hand-compacted sad outright unless makify recomputes it, and acdcmap(compactify=True) returns the expanded form unchanged. NOT established: how to build a d-bearing nested section correctly -- sectattr refused the input constructed for that test, which means the right API is unknown here, not that the invariant case fails. WHY IT MATTERS: award's index.md documents a three-layer compact/partial/full disclosure design, face-to-face and the SEDI family lean on disclosure too, and @jsmu322m decided the other ten schemas 'stay attributive' partly on the assumption that attributive graduated disclosure works. Award 2.0.0 therefore ships its layers as PROSE with no partial-disclosure example, the same disposition @sd2qfw took for sedi-id under the v1 oracle. This is a question for heti/keripy, not something to reverse-engineer further from this repo.
