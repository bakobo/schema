# Document and tighten GCD's chain of authority through e.issuer. Every GCD version (1.0.0, 2.0.1, 3.1.0, current) carries one required edge, e.issuer, operator I2I (this ACDC's issuer MUST be the issuee of the target), with s unpinned, so a GCD can point at the parent GCD that made its issuer a delegate, recursively, up to a root (e.g. a vLEI LE credential). That is GCD's chain of authority, arbitrarily long, but nothing says so: the schema describes the edge only as proving the issuer's IDENTITY, and gcd/index.md never mentions chains, parents, or re-delegation, so a reader (a research agent during the ppp survey, 2026-10-13) concluded GCD has no parent edge. Precision gaps: (1) no normative narrowing rule (a child's constraints must lie within its parent's; how a verifier intersects constraints along the chain, incl. targets, acts, time windows, exerciseMode authorize vs act); rules.json onlyDelegateHeldAuthority is soft ('reasonably believe', 'e.g., via edges'); (2) no guidance on chain depth or termination/root types; (3) how revocation of a parent affects descendants (verifier MUST check rd of every link?). Distinct from tick 5eok, which concerns relayed target designation (designatedBy), not delegation chains.
