# SEDI Age Attestation (`sedi-age`)

**Bakobo provisional profile, version 3.0.0.** Sam Smith has no age schema at pinned keripy commit `ec307cd74`; his proposed age mechanism in [discussion #1098](https://github.com/WebOfTrust/keripy/discussions/1098) may replace this aggregate of boolean thresholds. The old 2.0.0 schema remains resolvable in [`sedi-age-2.0.0/`](../sedi-age-2.0.0/).

The state issues this aggregate credential to the citizen. Its `A` section contains an issuee block, an as-of date, and blinded boolean blocks for thresholds 13, 16, 18, 21, 55, and 65. The holder can disclose the over-21 block without disclosing a birth date. The `identity` edge now requires `E1E` and pins its far schema to [SEDI Core](../sedi-core/). `E1E` relates the two credentials' issuees without requiring their issuers to match. This corrects the stale earlier claim that the age credential has no identity edge. The `s` and `r` sections accept compact SAIDs or expanded objects; the expanded rules object has Sam's `{d,l}` shape.

[`example.json`](example.json) is the full credential, and [`examples/prove-over-21.json`](examples/prove-over-21.json) discloses its over-21 block. [`invalid/`](invalid/) contains cases the schema refuses. The examples' `A` section was built with keripy's `Aggor`; the corpus linter checks schema and outer SAIDs but does not yet recompute the AGID (tick `7v2h`). The schema does not yet pin the AGID and issuee blocks to array positions (tick `6grq`); callers must verify the issuee binding with the protocol, not infer it from JSON Schema validation alone.

The rules SAID in `r` refers to the standalone [Bakobo governance artifact](../sedi-id/rules.json). Its legal text is separate from this credential schema. The choice of issuer and threshold mechanism remains subject to confirmation with Sam.
