# SEDI Guardianship (`sedi-guardian`)

The state issues this registry-bound credential to the guardian, naming the guardian as its issuee (`a.i`) and the ward by AID in the `a.ward` string. Its attributes are the guardian's `role`, the ward, an `issuedDate` block and an optional `expirationDate` block, and its only edge is a required `utahAgent` edge to an authorizing agent credential. There is no guardian-to-ward edge: the ward's [Core](../sedi-core/) carries a `guardians` edge group whose members reach guardianship credentials by `NI2I`. This is [Sam Smith's Guardianship schema at keripy `927c0cb8`](https://github.com/WebOfTrust/keripy/blob/927c0cb8/tests/sedi/test_sedi.py#L807-L960), reproduced byte for byte with SAID `EDJzXg34opr6-odFix6lpiccfHF7ksITHuIES_MQRH4b`. Sam Smith is the author. Status must be checked in the issuer's registry.

Sam's schema leaves `role` a free string and the `utahAgent` operator unconstrained, and it carries no scope or delegable powers. A verifier must check that the ward named here is the issuee of the Core whose `guardians` group reaches this credential. A [ward authorization](../sedi-ward-authz/) issued under this guardianship can be bounded only by the guardian's `role`, not by a list of powers.

[`example.json`](example.json) is Gal's guardianship of Wyn and [`examples/guy.json`](examples/guy.json) is Guy's, both parent guardianships from Sam's `test_sedi_acdcs` at `927c0cb8`, unmodified. Their `utahAgent` edges carry Sam's `DI2I` operator and name his Agent schema; those are his data, and the demo keeps its own stand-in agent schema. [`invalid/`](invalid/) covers a missing role, ward or issued date, a ward that is not a string, an extra attribute, a `utahAgent` edge with no far node, and missing or extra envelope fields.

Bakobo's provisional guardian profile, version 3.0.0, with `scope`, `powers`, `basis` and `recognition` attributes and `citizen` and `authorization` edges, is superseded and stays resolvable in [`sedi-guardian-pre-927c0cb8-3.0.0/`](../sedi-guardian-pre-927c0cb8-3.0.0/); version 2.0.0 stays in [`sedi-guardian-2.0.0/`](../sedi-guardian-2.0.0/). [`rules.json`](rules.json) is the 3.0.0 profile's governance artifact, kept at this path because the archived schemas name it; the [2.0.0 rules](../sedi-guardian-2.0.0/rules.json) remain resolvable. Sam's schema does not use it. `this.i` `@6ge27cya`, `@jmr6znql` and `@6uxg0vj0` record these decisions.
